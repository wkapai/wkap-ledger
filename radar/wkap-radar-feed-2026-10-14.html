SAVINGS-RETENTION SHEET — analytical calculation, not a measured
Microsoft result:
If matched useful output rises 40% at equal power, power per output
falls by 1−1/1.4 = 28.6%. Let E be baseline energy cost per useful
output in currency, ΔO the change in all other costs per output
including allocated development/deployment, and ΔP the revenue
concession per output. Conditional cost saving G=(2/7)×E−ΔO; retained
contribution change ΔM=G−ΔP. Positive ΔO means additional costs;
positive ΔP means a lower price. All amounts must use the same
unit/currency and comparison period. Neither 40% nor 28.6% is an
established all-in cost reduction. No energy share or actual
retained-savings amount is supplied here.
Model volume, fixed costs, investment and shares separately; no
invented usage forecast. Historical financial context:
https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/investor/events/fy-2026/earnings-fy-2026-q4
