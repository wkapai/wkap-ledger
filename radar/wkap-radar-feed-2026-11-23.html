SCENARIO_BRANCHES

Worksheet A — Resolved-call ledger, TWLO
Hold service quality and accepted outcomes constant. Record paid
calls, paid minutes per call, realized price, voice/speech/model
charges and support cost in their actual billing perimeter. Compare
the counterfactual without the new capability. A faster call can
reduce both expense and revenue; calculate retained contribution
rather than celebrating duration alone. Expansion branch: paid volume
and incremental services outweigh duration compression and cost.
Efficiency-only branch: the customer saves time while supplier revenue
per call falls. Cost-pressure branch: failover, support or discounts
absorb the benefit. Missing enterprise rates stay unavailable; do not
substitute the list price for a realized margin.

Worksheet B — Seat-offset bridge, FIVN
Split the same customer's renewal into retained human subscriptions,
removed subscriptions, new AI usage fees, services and
delivery/support costs. Compare contribution on the old and new
contract before adding acquisition expenses or purported synergies.
Expansion branch: incremental AI contribution exceeds lost seat
contribution and added costs. Flat branch: the billing unit changes
without improving owner economics. Contraction branch: reduced
subscriptions and support/integration investment overwhelm new AI
fees. Public bundle minimums do not prove each enterprise retains
every seat or pays the published rate.

Worksheet C — Model and human-exception test, NICE
Run the same quality-accepted task under default included models,
premium models and customer-owned model contracts. Track who pays each
model invoice, human consultation time, successful resolutions, repeat
contacts, transfers and collections. A lower transfer count can
coexist with longer human supervision or repeat calls. Expansion
branch: accepted production and contribution rise with controlled
exceptions. Procurement-only branch: the invoice is simpler but
retained contribution is unchanged. Cost-transfer branch: included
compute and exceptions grow faster than the bill. Keep Teams telephony
routing separate from Cognigy's automation receipt.

Worksheet D — Owner cash and pricing hurdle, all three
Reconcile group operating cash to equipment and capitalized
development, then separate acquisitions, financing, repurchases and
diluted ownership. Keep stock compensation visible even when cash flow
adds it back. For NICE, Q 3 operating cash USD 97.728m less equipment
USD 7.295m and capitalized software USD 21.419m equals USD 69.014m
before acquisitions and financing; this is a newsroom calculation from
the statement, not a promise of distributable cash. For TWLO, retain
the issuer's FCF definition and share-count countercheck. For FIVN, do
not treat operating cash as cash after the proposed acquisition.
Finally require an independently defensible expectations baseline,
specific mismatch, revealing catalyst and invalidator. No object
clears that pricing hurdle today.
