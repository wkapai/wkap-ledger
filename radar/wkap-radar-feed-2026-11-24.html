SCENARIO_BRANCHES

Worksheet A — Invoice after allowances. For each alternative record
task identifier, accepted outcome, billing period, contracted pool,
starting/ending allowance, eligible units, conversion rate,
credits/discounts, incremental charge and collection. Unknown is
unavailable. Unused capacity is not a negative invoice; using prepaid
capacity is not automatically new revenue. Confirm only an actual
contract perimeter, never a list-price conversion applied across
vendors.

Worksheet B — Rehearsal versus production. Run the same documented
class of workforce question through a permission-valid test and
production environment in customer evidence, without the newsroom
accessing customer systems. Record environment, entitlement, billable
actions/rows, excluded steps and quality. Workday's sandbox exclusion
and ServiceNow's eligible subproduction rule make this a concrete
contract comparison. It is a request for evidence, not permission to
deploy agents or obtain private HR data.

Worksheet C — Accepted-answer consumption. Separate model reasoning,
API messages, tools, executed SQL, retrieval and storage. Capture
retries, accepted answers and actual service charges. Compare
before/after efficiency at matched quality and demand; do not call
fewer retries a lost business unless retained contribution actually
falls. A useful agent may bring entirely new workloads, merely pull
forward existing work or optimize an unchanged workload. Those
branches have different revenue implications.

Worksheet D — Cash and ownership. Reconcile group operating cash,
equipment, capitalized development where applicable, acquisition cash,
share settlements and repurchases using matching periods. Keep
accounting stock compensation distinct from cash already spent and
from diluted ownership. Do not subtract it mechanically twice.
Separate recurring cash from adjustments, and avoid annualizing a
seasonal quarter. Group figures are a financial guardrail, not causal
product margins.

Confirm branch: source-backed paid expansion after entitlements plus
retained contribution/collection improves. Upgrade only the proven
component; a separate expectations baseline still controls stock
promotion.
Mixed branch: quality/adoption improves while consumption per task
falls or capacity remains prepaid. Keep the mechanism pending and use
Worksheets A/C to determine whether profitable new demand offsets
efficiency. Retrieval rank can change when a more decisive source
becomes available, with the reason logged.
Break branch: repeated paid evidence shows seat/fee compression,
optimization or integration/delivery costs outweigh incremental
receipts. Weaken or invalidate that capture component; a successful
demonstration does not repair it. Missing disclosure is NO_NEW_DATA,
preserving earlier verified states and unanswered fields, not a break
observation.

THESIS_SUMMARY
The enterprise buys a commercial entitlement and may pay for excess
eligible activity. Another model can supply intelligence while
ServiceNow governs and records actions. That does not mean all action
types are charged or each action produces a new invoice. Hosting,
support, acquisition cash, seats displaced and compensation costs
still determine what owners retain.

WKAP_ANGLE
The surface-level frame: The system of action governs the work, so it
necessarily owns a new toll on every agent action.
The alternative frame: Governance may support renewals or larger
contracts, while prepaid pools and excluded actions separate activity
growth from incremental receipts.
The key research question: Does governance retain profitable new
contracts or overages after seats, allowances and costs?
