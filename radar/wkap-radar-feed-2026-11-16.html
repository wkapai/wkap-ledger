An agent can place a new order, reroute the order you already would
have placed, or execute a payment you already approved. Those are
three different economic events. Uber's early counts, United's access
rules and Visa's invoice pilot let a reader test them without assuming
an integration is a new customer or a stock opportunity. The new

settle merchant permission, fees or retained profit.
