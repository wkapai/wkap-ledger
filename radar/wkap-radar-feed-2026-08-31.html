AI capex is widening from accelerators toward systems that cool,
connect, power, and feed them. That creates opportunity and adjacency
risk. SLB has the strongest evidence because Kelvion already has
revenue, EBITDA, and meaningful data-center exposure. ARRY has a
completed bolt-on and a disclosed accretion expectation, but AWM
data-center revenue is not quantified. ZDGE has early commercial
signals and existing free cash flow, but DataSeeds remains early and
the proposed financing includes 90% warrant coverage.

Part 2 — Today’s Thesis Objects

SLB: Approximately $3.4 billion cash plus $0.7 billion assumed debt
buys Kelvion, which is expected to generate $2.3 billion to $2.4
billion of 2026 revenue and $350 million to $400 million of adjusted
EBITDA. Data centers are expected to contribute $1.2 billion to $1.3
billion. SLB targets $4.5 billion to $5.0 billion of combined
data-center revenue in 2028.

ARRY: ARRAY closed AWM for approximately $165 million of cash at
closing, net of adjustments. The original agreement described
approximately $203 million of total potential consideration. AWM had
nearly $60 million of trailing-twelve-month revenue, and ARRAY expects
at least high-single-digit adjusted EPS accretion in year one before
synergies.

ZDGE: Zedge announced an approximately $7.5 million insider-led
proposed investment with 90% warrant coverage. DataSeeds fulfilled its
first six-figure order and signed its first model-evaluation deal.
Fiscal Q3 free cash flow was $1.2 million, up 55%, with $19.7 million
of cash and no debt.

Part 3 — Attention Flow Today

For SLB, the dispute is whether a roughly $4.1 billion transaction
including assumed debt creates a credible cooling platform or
integration risk. For ARRY, it is whether a closed solar
balance-of-system deal delivers accretion and BESS or data-center
wins. For ZDGE, it is whether early DataSeeds proof justifies dilutive
capital. KOL posts identify these disputes; filings and results
resolve them.

Part 4 — The Better Question

The surface question is: “Which non-chip company has the best AI angle?”

The better question is: “Which object has the shortest path from
today’s evidence to recurring revenue, profit, and free cash flow
after integration costs, capital needs, and dilution?”
