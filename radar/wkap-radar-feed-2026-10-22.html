Incremental capability 1 — CAPITAL-INSTRUMENT LEDGER
For each instrument record authorization/effective/closing date;
proposed versus issued; cash actually received;
common/preferred/mandatory-convertible rights; dividends/conversion
terms; voting rights; share count; compensation/acquisition/capital
use; and source. Meta's future 1:1 dividend starts with zero outside
proceeds from the dividend itself, not zero corporate liquidity.
Alphabet's 49.562 bn belongs in received proceeds; its unused 40 bn
does not. Do not assume mandatory preferred is economically equivalent
to ordinary common or subtract proceeds from capex as if they were
operating revenue. Price and use of later issuance can create or
destroy value; dilution alone is not the verdict.

Incremental capability 2 — CASH-PERIMETER WORKSHEET

equipment sales/incentives, lease principal, issuer FCF, calculated
residual and reconciliation difference. Meta is quarterly with lease
principal; Alphabet's reported bridge is quarterly PPE; Amazon's cited
bridge is TTM with net equipment. Do not directly rank those three
residuals as identical cash margins. Retrieve matching
periods/definitions where available; leave unavailable cells
unmatched. Reconcile consolidated liquidity separately from internally
generated cash and financing proceeds, avoiding a second count of the
same receipt.

Incremental capability 3 — EARNINGS-QUALITY AND INCREMENTAL-RETURN TEST

EPS subtraction. Never subtract a pretax dollar amount from after-tax
EPS. Build an explicit incremental operating-cash scenario after new
investment, maintenance, financing costs and diluted shares. Missing
attribution remains UNAVAILABLE. Compare plausible downside duration
and earning power to a dated share price before calling a bargain; no
unsupported target or current normalized multiple is supplied.
