WKAP_ANGLE

“Wolfspeed is a distressed EV silicon-carbide manufacturer.”

The alternative frame:

“Wolfspeed may own scarce U.S. power-semiconductor capacity that becomes

systems, where efficiency, voltage handling, and thermal performance matter.

The rerating case requires more than strategic relevance. AI data-center
revenue must become large enough to improve fab utilization, product mix,
gross margins, and confidence in the capital structure.
