## SCENARIO_BRANCHES

CONFIRM — Paid commercial evidence appears, accepted deployments
convert to collections, and net profit after displaced content/capital
remains attractive. Upgrade only the confirmed component; re-rank by
remaining uncertainty. A customer efficiency improvement alone is
insufficient. Strong execution may still be fully priced.

MIXED — Engineering improves but product margins remain undisclosed;
acquired scale increases while organic contribution or cash conversion
is less clear. Retain PENDING commercial/return extensions and
SUPPORTED historical facts. Move the next research hour to the missing
field, not automatically to the stock with the best price chart.

BREAK — Qualification fails, displacement erodes profit without
adequate replacement, or deployment delays combine with worsening
collections. Downgrade the affected future mechanism. Invalidate only
when primary evidence contradicts the specified durable-return route;
preserve already-reported cash and avoid alleging default or
manipulation.

NO_NEW_DATA — Silence or another presentation is not a failed
deployment. Retain component status, timestamp the completed search
and set the next actual calendar/source checkpoint. Do not recycle an
expired event as an upcoming catalyst.
