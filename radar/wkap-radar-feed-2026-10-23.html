## CROSS_OBJECT_EVIDENCE_MATRIX

Worksheet A — Capacity, price and mix

REPORTED_FACT WDC: revenue 2818→4381 million dollars and total
capacity 204→248 exabytes, matching year-earlier fiscal quarters in
the current deck. INTERPRETATION: capacity +21.57%, revenue +55.46%;
(4381/248)/(2818/204)−1 = +27.88% blended revenue per exabyte. This is
not matched-product ASP. Fill product/end-market mix, nearline versus
other capacity, same-product prices and cost per capacity from the
original company material; absent cells stay unavailable. Do not adopt
a KOL's call attribution without that call.

Use the multiplicative bridge: revenue ratio = capacity ratio ×
blended revenue-per-capacity ratio. A hypothetical 20% capacity gain
and 10% lower blended revenue per capacity produce 8% revenue growth,
not 10%. This is a sensitivity, not guidance. It demonstrates why
physical growth can remain healthy while revenue decelerates. New
product density can also increase capacity without proportional unit
growth; qualify before estimating motor/head demand or third-party
orders.

MANAGEMENT_EXPECTATION: Mozaic/HAMR strategy and July guide.

per unit, qualification, product mix and price? REPORTED_FACT SNDK:
